About WA Tech Engineering

Perth's trusted partner for electrical and electronic engineering — built on innovation, precision, and a passion for technology.

Our Story

Who We Are

WA Tech Engineering is a Perth-based engineering services company providing electrical and electronic design, consulting, and smart technology solutions to businesses, startups, and builders across Western Australia.

We believe that great engineering should be accessible to every business — not just the big players. That's why we work with small startups, medium businesses, service companies, and builders to deliver smart, practical, and affordable engineering solutions tailored to their specific needs.

From electrical design and smart home automation to industrial PLC/SCADA systems and PCB prototyping, our team brings deep technical expertise and a genuine commitment to your success — every step of the way.

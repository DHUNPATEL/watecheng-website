FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service?+
We are based in Hillarys, Perth and primarily service the greater Perth metropolitan area and surrounds. We can also assist clients across Western Australia for design and consulting work remotely.
How much does a consultation cost?+
We offer a free initial consultation for all new enquiries. This allows us to understand your project requirements before providing a detailed, no-obligation quote.
Do you work with small businesses and startups?+
Absolutely — this is one of our specialties. We believe great engineering should be accessible to everyone, so we offer scalable solutions to suit any budget. Whether you need a PCB prototype, a smart home system, or electrical design, we'll find an approach that works for you.
Are you licensed for electrical works?+
For physical electrical installation work, we partner with a network of fully licensed and insured electrical contractors. Our engineering team handles design, consulting, and project management, while our trusted partners carry out the on-site electrical works.
How long does a typical project take?+
Project timelines vary significantly depending on the scope and complexity. A PCB prototype might take 2–4 weeks, while a full home automation or PLC automation project could take 6–12 weeks. We'll provide a clear timeline estimate as part of your quote.
Can you help with solar system design?+
Yes! We provide solar PV system design, energy auditing, and installation coordination for residential and commercial clients across Perth. We'll assess your energy usage, design the right system size, and coordinate with accredited installers to get it done.
